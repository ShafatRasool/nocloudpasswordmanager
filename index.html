Privacy Policy

No Cloud - Password Manager

Designed for complete privacy. Your passwords stay on your device—always.

🛡 Privacy at a Glance

  • Your passwords stay on your device.
  • No cloud storage or account required.
  • Your encrypted vault is never uploaded.
  • Encrypted backups remain on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • Google AdMob is used to display ads.
🔐

Data Storage

All passwords, notes, categories, and settings are securely encrypted and stored locally on your device. Your information is never uploaded to our servers.

💾

Backup & Restore

When you create a backup, an encrypted backup file is saved only to the location you choose. The app never uploads or accesses your backup.

📡

Internet Usage

Password Manager No Cloud works completely offline. An internet connection is not required to store or access your passwords.

🔑

Security

Access to your vault is protected by your Master Password or your device's built-in authentication. Only you can unlock your stored information.

Information Collection

Password Manager No Cloud does not collect, store, or transmit your passwords, notes, categories, or encrypted vault to us.

To support the free version of the app, Google AdMob is used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect limited device information, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

We never receive or have access to your passwords or encrypted data.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect certain device information to provide and measure ads.

For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy .

Your passwords, notes, encrypted vault, and backup files are never shared with Google AdMob or with us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page along with the latest revision date.
